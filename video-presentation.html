PHYSICAL PAYLOAD ENGINEERING

How The Hardware Is Built

Physical Assembly Breakdown

The TARANG payload is built as a self-contained, modular cylindrical pod designed for standard AUV payload bays (5.5" hull slot). The entire digital and analog electronics stack is isolated into three layered PCBs to eliminate EMI coupling between high-voltage acoustic drivers and sensitive analog hydrophone inputs.

Subsystem Hardware Component Role & Engineering Function
Main MCU STM32H743ZI (ARM Cortex-M7 @ 480MHz) Ingests real-time sensor ADC, manages DDS wave synthesis, streams DAC via DMA channel.
DAC Converter 12-Bit 1 MSPS Internal / MCP4922 SPI Converts synthesized waveform samples into raw stepped analog voltage signal.
Analog Filter 4th-Order Sallen-Key Butterworth LPF Anti-aliasing smoothing; removes DAC quantization staircase steps before amplification.
Power Driver OPA1612 + Toroidal Matching Transformer Steps up 12V rail to 80Vpp AC driving voltage matched to 50Ω transducer impedance.
Acoustic Head PZT-5H Piezo-Ceramic Transducer Disc Emits mechanical acoustic pressure wave pulses into water column (100–500 kHz).
Enclosure Carbon-Fiber Tube + CNC Al Endcaps IP68 pressure housing rated to 200m depth with SubConn marine wet-mate connectors.
MISSION PROFILES IN ACTION

How It Behaves in Real Marine Scenarios

SCENARIO A · HIGH TURBIDITY

Entering Muddy River Estuary

Suspended mud and silt particles cause Rayleigh scattering of high-frequency sound. Traditional 500 kHz sonars black out completely.

Center Frequency: 120 kHz (Low penetration)
Pulse Width: 6.0 ms (High energy)
Window Function: Blackman (-58 dB sidelobes)
Acoustic Output: Max Power (100%)
SCENARIO B · SHALLOW REEF

Clear Shallow Water Coral Reef

Shallow water causes strong multipath boundary reflections from the surface. Water is crystal clear; ultra-fine millimeter resolution is demanded.

Center Frequency: 450 kHz (High resolution)
Pulse Width: 0.8 ms (Short burst)
Modulation: Geometric Wideband Sweep
Acoustic Output: 30% Power (Battery saving)
SCENARIO C · DEEP TRENCH

Deep Abyss & Thermocline Layer

High hydrostatic pressure and severe acoustic bending across temperature thermoclines. Long ranges (>500m) require optimal compression.

Center Frequency: 100 kHz
Pulse Code: 13-Chip Barker Phase Code
Matched Filter Gain: +11.1 dB SNR improvement
Energy Conservation: Sleep mode between pings
EXPERIMENTAL PROOF

Bench Validation & FFT Spectrum Analysis

Testing on the Judging Bench

The physical analog output of the transmitter payload board is connected directly to a digital storage oscilloscope and RF spectrum analyzer via BNC coaxial test probes.

  • ✔ Smooth Window Envelope: Digital Hamming windowing eliminates sharp dV/dt transient spikes at ping onset, protecting the transducer ceramic from mechanical fracturing.
  • ✔ Clean FFT Spectrogram: The 4th-order Butterworth low-pass filter suppresses DAC clock harmonics below -50 dB, ensuring pure acoustic radiation.
  • ✔ Zero CPU Stall: Logic analyzer traces verify that the STM32 DMA controller transfers 4096-word buffers with 0% CPU blocking, allowing real-time sensor processing.
