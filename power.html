⚡ Dynamic Gain & Amplitude Scaling
Dynamically dials down transmit driver voltage in high-reflectivity or shallow zones where SNR exceeds the 20 dB processing threshold.

〰️ Chirp Duration Modulation
Contracts pulse width from 12 ms down to 4 ms in low-reverberation regimes while maintaining matched-filter pulse compression gain.
Efficiency Gain: ~9.2% avg
💤 Inter-Ping Power Gating
Puts the high-voltage analog transmit bridge into ultra-low-leakage sleep during the 992 ms acoustic listen and idle window.
Efficiency Gain: ~4.2% avg
